# Module 3: Governance, Process & Evolution

[← Back to Main Index](../README.md)

---

### 1. Process-Oriented System Evolution
* **Architectural Reference:** Lesson 26 (*Focus on process rather than product*)
* **Principle:** Value resides in the quality and adaptability of the design method rather than static early deliverables.
* **IT Application:** Prioritize robust automated deployment pipelines, continuous testing, and automated security scanning over static, upfront design artifacts.

---

### 2. Objectivity and Consistency
* **Architectural Reference:** Lessons 12, 13, 15 (*Grids, alignments, and objective rules*)
* **Principle:** Dependability relies on explicit, objective visual grids rather than arbitrary artistic preference.
* **IT Application:** Replace subjective code review disputes with automated ArchUnit rules, linters, and static security analyzers.

---

### 3. Courage to Retire Legacy Components
* **Architectural Reference:** Lesson 44 (*Don't fall in love with your ideas*)
* **Principle:** Abandon sub-components that no longer align with total system requirements, regardless of historical investment.
* **IT Application:** Actively decommission bespoke legacy utilities in favor of widely maintained industry standards to reduce technical debt.

---

### 4. The Arrival Sequence
* **Architectural Reference:** Lesson 62 (*The arrival sequence sets expectations...*)
* **Principle:** The transition into a space establishes operational mental models and trust.
* **IT Application:** Standardize developer onboarding, CLI output formats, and initial API authentication handshakes to establish system reliability immediately.
